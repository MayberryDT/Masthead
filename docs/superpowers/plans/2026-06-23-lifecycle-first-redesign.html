Detail pattern

Card click opens a modal, not an expanded grid card

The modal can carry all evidence without making the board noisy.

Why modal wins here

1

Board stays scannable

Cards can stay small and lifecycle-focused while details move into a deliberate inspection surface.

2

Review state is contextual

Dismissed or reviewed becomes a note in the modal, not the runtime status on the card.

3

Evidence can stay complete

Raw evidence references, timeline, changed files, and classifier output still exist, just not front-and-center.

LLM role

Use a small fast model for why it ended, not whether it is running

A

Deterministic source of truth

Running, idle, and ended come from hook events, timestamps, terminal events, and observer state.

B

Evidence-backed inference

A future model receives redacted metadata and returns a strict outcome candidate with evidence refs.

C

Visible uncertainty

Low-confidence candidates show as inferred context and never become urgent P0 alerts.
