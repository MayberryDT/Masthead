Settings layout exploration

Five focused iterations of the channel rack layout

Each option uses the same Settings content and imports the current app card and control styling from the live stylesheets. The only prototype changes here are spatial arrangement, grouping, density, and responsive behavior.

01

Four Lane Rack

Four narrow vertical channels keep every card readable while allowing Storage, policy, session capture, and MCP to scan as separate lanes.

4 columns / stacked lanes
02

Priority Bay

MCP becomes the center bay without becoming a full-width panel. Lower-frequency storage and danger controls sit in slimmer guard rails.

5 columns / centered MCP
03

Paired Decks

Three balanced decks group policy and enrichment on the left, durable storage in the middle, and agent access on the right.

3 columns / paired stacks
04

Offset Rail

A left rail carries database facts and destructive actions while hooks form a tall operational spine beside the MCP card.

4 columns / offset stacks
05

Stepped Rack

A denser, masonry-like rack uses short cards as steps around the tall MCP bay, reducing the blocky feeling of equal columns.

5 columns / staggered rhythm
